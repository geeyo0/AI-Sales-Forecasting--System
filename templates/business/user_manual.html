FoodCast Help Center

Find simple instructions and answers about using FoodCast.

Showing all help topics

1

Getting started

  1. Open FoodCast.
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. Enter the code sent to your email.
  4. Select Sign in.

After signing in, the Home page shows a quick look at your business.

2

Using the Home page

The Home page shows:

  • Sales for today
  • Sales for this week
  • Sales for this month
  • Your best-selling products
  • Products that need more stock
3

Adding products

  1. Select Products from the side menu.
  2. Select Add product.
  3. Enter the product name.
  4. Choose its category and selling unit.
  5. Enter its price.
  6. Select Save product.

Use the edit button to change a product. Select several products to edit or delete them together.

4

Updating stock

  1. Select Stock from the side menu.
  2. Select Update stock.
  3. Choose a product.
  4. Choose whether stock was added or removed.
  5. Enter the amount.
  6. Select Save.

Check the amount carefully before saving. This helps FoodCast show the correct stock.

5

Recording sales

  1. Select Sales from the side menu.
  2. Select Record sales.
  3. Choose the product and date.
  4. Enter how many were sold.
  5. Check the total amount.
  6. Select Save sales.

Record sales regularly so your forecast has enough information to use.

6

Creating a sales forecast

  1. Select Sales forecast.
  2. Choose 1, 7, or 30 days.
  3. Select Generate forecast.
  4. Wait for the forecast to finish.
  5. Review the expected sales and stock needs.

A forecast is an estimate. Continue recording daily sales to help improve future results.

7

Updating your location

  1. Open Sales forecast.
  2. Select Update location.
  3. Allow location access when asked.
  4. Wait for the weather information to update.

Weather information helps you consider possible changes in customer visits.

8

Changing your account information

  1. Select your profile picture.
  2. Select Edit profile.
  3. Change the information you need.
  4. Select Save changes.

A code may be sent to your email when you change important account information.

9

If you forgot your password

  1. Open the Sign in page.
  2. Select Forgot password.
  3. Enter your email address.
  4. Enter the code sent to your email.
  5. Create a new password.
10

Common problems

I did not receive an email code

Check your Spam folder. Make sure the email address is correct, then try again.

The forecast has no results

Add products and record sales regularly. The forecast needs past sales before it can show useful results.

My stock amount looks wrong

Open Stock and check the recent updates. Add a correction using Update stock.

A page does not update

Refresh the page. If the problem continues, sign out and sign in again.
